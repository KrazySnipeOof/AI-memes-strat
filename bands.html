RETURN BANDS

Every book sorted by the multiple its exit ladder can actually deliver, not by ambition. Ladders are read from each book's config file at request time, so this page re-bands a book the moment you change its stop or cap in EDIT PARAMETERS. Expectancy is the mean exit multiple per trade net of the 4% cost model; 1.000x is breakeven.

How a book gets its band. reach = the hard take-profit, the most one trade can ever return. target = the first rung that actually sells something, or reach when no rung does — a rung with sell_fraction_of_remaining: 0 banks nothing, it only arms the trailing stop, which is why BOUNCE, HOLDER and HOMERUN are ride-to-the-cap books despite showing a low rung in their config. Then: 10x+ if reach ≥ 10 · 2–5x if target ≥ 2.0 or the measured share of trades reaching 2x is ≥ 20% · 2x otherwise. The second leg exists because a cap is permission, not outcome: GRIND-VWAP-X is capped at 3.0x and has never printed above 1.78x in 126 trades. That is why ≥2x and Max are shown next to the cap rather than folded into it.

Ladders: each book's live config (main tree and both worktrees), read fresh on every request — the same source the per-book pages use.
Measured columns: recomputed from the raw exit multiples in the reports/ file named on each row, not copied from its summary block. Books with no row there (BASE, BOUNCE, HOLDER) have no backtest on the ladder they are currently running; their research numbers were produced on a different exit ladder and are deliberately not quoted here.
Live columns: closed paper round trips only — voids and open positions excluded, exactly as the SORTIE RECORD tiles treat them. Paper trading only; no live capital.
